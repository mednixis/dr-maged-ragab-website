About the Professor

Prof. Maged M. Ragab, MD

Professor of Urology and Andrology and Head of the Andrology Unit at Tanta University Hospital. Subspecialty: male infertility, andrology and endourology.

He has taught urology to medical students since 1998, serves as Secretary of the Erectile Dysfunction section of the Egyptian Urological Association, and is vice chairman of the Egyptian Urological Guidelines committee for male infertility.
